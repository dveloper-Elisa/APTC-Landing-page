About Us

APTC Ltd is an investment Holding Trust Company whose offices are located in the heart of Kigali City. It was legally registered under registration code No 103454201 in accordance with the Rwanda company law on the 24th day of October 2014. APTC has invested in a wide range of business activities in three subsidiary companies and two stand-alone projects, such as Agro Processing Industries (API) Ltd, Rugari Meat Processing Ltd, Nyanza Milk Industries Ltd, Fertilizer, and Seed Distribution... Our subsidiaries

Agro processing Industries

Agro processing Industries is engaged in crop production with particular focus on maize, beans and soya beans.

Rugali Meat Processing industries

Rugari Meat Processing Ltd is also a subsidiary company under APTC with independent management team that runs its day-to-day business activities. It is fully engaged in animal rearing and meat processing activities.

Nyanza Milk Industries

Nyanza Milk Industries it process milk into a variety of products such Pasteurized milk ( Inshyushyu),curd milk ( Ikivuguto) yoghurt in strawberry, vanilla and mixed fruits flavors and cheese in varying flavors, types and shapes.
